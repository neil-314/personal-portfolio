## Selected work

### IncomeLens

An income analytics prototype that turns simulated bank transactions into monthly summaries, recurring income sources, and visual trends. Its data pipeline makes the classification rules visible so users can inspect how the results were produced.

Python · FastAPI · Plaid Sandbox · Data analysis

### Monte Carlo Portfolio Risk & Derivatives Engine

A Python project exploring portfolio outcomes under different volatility and correlation assumptions. It combines risk measures and stress scenarios with European option pricing and comparisons against Black–Scholes.

Python · NumPy · Pandas · SciPy · Matplotlib

### TVM Study Desk

A learning workspace for time value of money, with short lessons, practice problems, worked explanations, and a calculator trainer. The focus is helping learners understand the cash flows and inputs behind an answer.

Financial mathematics · Interactive learning · Cash-flow modelling

### One Piece app

An interactive crew-building project inspired by One Piece. Players place characters into specialist roles, explore scoring combinations, and refine their crews.

Working project title; use the current product name after checking the repository.

### Neil’s Photos

A collection of my wildlife, landscape, coastline, and architecture photography. I took and edited every image. The gallery brings the photographs together by subject, with location details alongside each frame.

Photography · Editing · C11 · HTML/CSS
